## MASTER COMMON LOCK

Use the style anchor only as a visual reference. Match its thin, slightly rough charcoal linework, off-white fibrous paper, matte low-saturation color washes, subtle uniform grain, restrained adult editorial finish, and believable object geometry. Never copy the anchor's table, drawer, window, room, or composition into another card.

The illustration must remain open-ended and observational. It may invite association, but it must not define a diagnosis, emotion, relationship meaning, or correct interpretation.

### STYLE

- sophisticated adult editorial illustration
- hand-drawn thin charcoal line
- tactile imperfect edges
- matte flat color washes
- restrained vintage print texture
- quiet negative space
- low saturation and controlled contrast

### PALETTE

### ABSOLUTE EXCLUSIONS

- text, letters, numbers, labels, pseudo-writing
- logo, signature, watermark, caption
- decorative card border or UI
- photorealism, stock-photo look
- 3D render, glossy finish
- children's-book or therapy-workbook tone
- explanatory icons or fixed symbolic meaning
- melodrama, sentimental glow, excessive detail
- duplicated objects, impossible perspective, broken anatomy
